# Joint Route-and-Bunker Planner Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Make the route answer know the fuel on board, plan a bunker stop with a quote, and show the best route ignoring fuel beside the recommended plan. The plan is route, speed, stop and quote, and keeps every grade above its margin. The alert and the bunker answer's reachability check use the same fuel calculation. Approvals link the instruction to its purchase order.

**Architecture:**
- **Domain.** One pure walk over 20 nm sub-steps (`packages/domain/src/track.ts`) feeds both the optimiser's costs and a new fuel-on-board projection.
- **Stop plans.** These are via-port polylines built in `packages/db` and evaluated in the optimiser over the speed grid, with a stop timeline computed in domain.
- **API.** It only wires inputs (weather, congestion mode, quotes, approved orders) and turns results into facts.
- **Approvals.** The dependency between an instruction and its order is derived from the stop's quote, never stored, and resolved by one shared function.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript 6 ESM, Zod (`packages/shared`), Vitest, Hono (`apps/api`), Drizzle + Postgres (`packages/db`), React 19 + Vite + Tailwind 4 + shadcn + MapLibre (`apps/web`), Playwright (`e2e/demo.spec.ts`).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-17-joint-route-bunker-design.md` (approved 2026-11-17). Read it before any task. The section numbers below ("spec §N") point into it.

   - there is no recommended plan;

   Tested in Task 4 (`plan` is null) and Task 10 (tray copy).
2. **A fleet vessel with no open requirement, or no weather snapshot.** Expected:
   - no stop section;
   - the plan is the best route when it keeps the margins, otherwise null with "runs short of <grade>";
   - the projection runs in calm water.

   Tested in Task 4 and Task 7.
3. **An approved order whose port the sea graph cannot route through** (a `RouteError` on either half). Expected: that stop plan reports `no_sea_route` and the rest of the route plan still returns. Tested in Task 5.

5. **A port outside the weather grid, or a stop after the forecast ends.** Expected: bunkering counts as workable, `beyondForecast: true`, and the tray says "weather beyond the forecast". Tested in Task 3 and Task 10.

### Task 4: Fuel checks, stop plans and the recommended plan in the optimiser

**Files:**
- Modify: `packages/domain/src/route-optimiser.ts`, `packages/domain/src/route-optimiser.test.ts`, `packages/domain/src/index.ts` (`DOMAIN_CODE_VERSION = "0.7.0"`)
- Create: `packages/domain/src/stop-plan.ts`, `packages/domain/src/stop-plan.test.ts`

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: Tasks 1–3.
- Produces:
  - `optimiseRouteSpeed(input: RouteSpeedInput): RouteSpeedResult` keeps its signature. Its result now carries `fuel` per option, `stopPlans` and `plan`.
  - `export function evaluateStopPlan(input: RouteSpeedInput, stop: StopCandidate, speedKn: number): StopPlanResult`
- **Rules (spec §3–§4):**
  - **No-stop options.**
    - When `input.fuel` is set, each option's `fuel` is `projectFuelAlongTrack` with the option's track and speed, and `stop: null`.
    - `insufficient_fuel` is added when `!keepsMargins`, and `feasible` covers all reasons.
    - **The fuel-blind choice is unchanged:** per-candidate speed and `bestCandidateId` are picked with `passes = no misses_arrive_by and no crosses_hazard`. Savings stay best vs baseline.
  - **Stop plans.**
    - One per `input.stops` entry, at its best speed on the grid. Ranking: feasible first, then `totalCostUsd`, then the earlier ETA, then the lower speed.
    - A candidate with `routeError` gives `{ feasible: false, reasons: ["no_sea_route"] }`. Its numbers are 0 and `fuel` and `stop` are null; no speeds are searched.
  - **Stop plan costs.**
    - Steaming fuel is priced at `fuelPriceUsdPerMt` with `etsCoverage`.
    - Stay fuel is priced at the same reference price, with coverage `portIsEu ? 1 : 0` for both ETS and FuelEU.
    - Time cost: `dailyCostUsd × max(hours, untilWindowHours) / 24`.
    - `voyageCostUsd = fuel + time + ETS + FuelEU`.
    - `bunkerFuelCostUsd = qtyMt × pricePerMt × fxToUsd`; `bargeFeeUsd = bargeFee × fxToUsd`.
    - `totalCostUsd = voyageCostUsd + bunkerFuelCostUsd + bargeFeeUsd`.
  - **Stop plan reasons:**
    - `insufficient_fuel` (`!keepsMargins`);
    - `exceeds_tank_capacity` (fuel on board of `grade` at stop arrival + `qtyMt` > `tankCapacityMt[grade]`);
    - `insufficient_quantity` (`offeredQtyMt < qtyMt`);
    - `outside_delivery_window` (arrival before `deliveryFrom` or after `deliveryTo`, when given);
    - `misses_arrive_by`;
    - `crosses_hazard`.
  - **`plan`:**
    - With stops: the cheapest feasible stop plan (`kind: "stop"`), or null when none is feasible.
    - With no stops: the fuel-blind best when it is feasible with fuel (`kind: "route"`, `addedHours: 0`, `addedUsd: 0`, `next: null`), else null.
    - `next` is the cheapest other feasible stop plan, with `marginUsd = next.total − plan.total`.
