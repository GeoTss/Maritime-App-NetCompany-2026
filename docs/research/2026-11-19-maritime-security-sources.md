# Maritime security sources: Fujairah → Rotterdam and danger areas

Researched 2026-11-19. Each bullet comes from the source cited at its end. "(secondary)" marks a firm or outlet quoting an official body. ukmto.org returns HTTP 403 to fetches, so I could not read JMIC's own PDFs from after 2 June 2026.

## Summary

- The JWC lists the whole Suez option as one area, from Fujairah (the UAE is a named country) to the Red Sea south of 25.5°N. The Gulf of Guinea is listed as its own polygon.
- JMIC threat levels in November 2026 (secondary): Bab el-Mandeb/Southern Red Sea and Gulf of Aden SUBSTANTIAL, Somali coast/basin MODERATE, Gulf of Oman MODERATE, Hormuz SEVERE.
- Active drivers: Houthi attacks on Saudi-linked ships since about 20 Jul 2026, the US/Israel–Iran conflict since 28 Feb 2026, and Somali hijackings again in 2026. The Gulf of Guinea had 2 incidents in January–September 2026.
- Costs are thinly sourced (see Gaps).

## 1. Red Sea / Bab-el-Mandeb / Gulf of Aden

- **JWC boundary.** The JWC area "Persian/Arabian Gulf, Gulf of Oman, Indian Ocean, Gulf of Aden and Southern Red Sea" is bounded:
  - NW: "by the Red Sea, south of Latitude 25.5°N, (not including Egyptian territorial waters)".
  - NE: from the Pakistan coast at 25°19'15"N 65°E.
  - E: to 10°48'N 65°E, then 10°48'N 60°15'E, then 6°45'S 48°45'E.
  - SW: from the Somalia border at 1°40'S 41°34'E to 6°45'S 48°45'E.

  Djibouti, Eritrea, Somalia, Sudan, Yemen and Saudi Arabia are also named countries (their ports and waters to 12 nm). [JWLA-035 Listed Areas, LMA/IUA Joint War Committee, 16 Sep 2026, https://lmalloyds.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/JWLA-035-Black-Sea.pdf]
- **HRA, 2015.** Red Sea northern limit 15°N, Gulf of Oman northern limit 22°N, eastern limit 65°E, southern limit 5°S. The MSCHOA registration area was "bounded by the Strait of Hormuz and Suez to the North, 10S and 78E". [Circular Letter 3606, IMO, 2 Dec 2015, https://wwwcdn.imo.org/localresources/en/OurWork/Security/Documents/Circular%20Letter%20No.3606.pdf]
- **HRA from 1 Sep 2021.** The polygon ran:
  - A: 01°30'S on the Somali coast
  - B: 01°30'S 046°00'E
  - C: 01°00'N 049°30'E
  - D: 09°00'N 055°00'E
  - E: 14°20'N 057°30'E
  - F: Yemen coast at 053°00'E
  - G: Yemeni Red Sea coast at 15°00'N
  - H: Eritrean border at 15°00'N
  - I: Djibouti/Somalia border

  [Technical Circular 048/2021, IRClass, 2021, https://irclass.org/technical-circulars/reduction-in-geographic-boundaries-of-hra-from-1-september-2021-reg]
- **HRA removed** at 00:01 UTC on 1 Jan 2023. The UKMTO Voluntary Reporting Area (VRA) "has not changed", and BMP5 reporting stays. [HRA to be removed on 1 January, Hellenic Shipping News/BIMCO, 20 Dec 2022, https://www.hellenicshippingnews.com/indian-ocean-high-risk-area-for-piracy-to-be-removed-on-1-january/]
- **VRA.** All reporting centres use the VRA on UKHO Chart Q6099. UKMTO is the primary contact, MSCIO the secondary. [Voluntary Reporting Guidance, MSCIO/UKMTO/CMF, Jan 2025, https://mscio.eu/documents/100/Voluntary_Reporting_Guidance_in_the_Indian_Ocean_and_Red_Sea_Region.pdf]
- **31 May 2026.**
  - Regional level CRITICAL.
  - Bab el-Mandeb/Southern Red Sea MODERATE, "No confirmed maritime attacks". Gulf of Aden MODERATE.
  - Bab el-Mandeb had 123 transits in 72 h, "still below pre-2023 baseline".

  [Update 052 to JMIC Advisory Note, JMIC, 31 May 2026, https://mscio.eu/media/documents/Update_052_JMIC_Advisory_Note_31_May.pdf]
- **20 Jul 2026.** The Houthis announced a blockade on Saudi Arabia. *Encelia* was hit, and Bab el-Mandeb transits fell from 41 to 29 in a day. [Shipping insurance rates rising, Al Jazeera, 23 Jul 2026, https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/7/23/how-shipping-insurance-rates-are-rising-as-hormuz-bab-al-mandeb-shut-down]
- **24 Aug 2026.** Bahri tanker *Amzan* was hit about 63 nm west of Yanbu. [Marine Insight, 25 Aug 2026, https://www.marineinsight.com/fire-breaks-out-on-saudi-oil-tanker-near-red-sea-port-of-yanbu-after-houthi-attack/]
- **2–9 Nov 2026 (secondary).** JMIC rates Bab el-Mandeb/Southern Red Sea and the Gulf of Aden SUBSTANTIAL. No merchant-ship attacks were reported. [Maritime Security Report 2–9 Nov 2026, Palaemon Maritime, 9 Nov 2026, https://palaemonmaritime.com/post/maritime-security-report-2nd-9th-november-2026]
- **Late October (secondary).** The Houthis "have not imposed a blanket closure". [Shafaq News citing UKMTO, 25 Oct 2026, https://shafaq.com/en/World/UKMTO-Hormuz-shipping-slumps-Bab-Al-Mandab-holds-steady]
- **Naval.** EUNAVFOR ASPIDES "shall end on 28 February 2027". [Council Decision (CFSP) 2026/439, EUR-Lex, 23 Feb 2026, https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dec/2026/439/oj/eng]

## 2. Somali Basin / Western Indian Ocean / Arabian Sea

- **Boundary.** The same JWC area applies, and Somalia is a named country. The HRA history above also applies.
- **IMB 2025.** "Only a small number" of incidents off Somalia, two of them far from shore in November. [IMB 2025 media release, ICC IMB, 15 Jan 2026, https://www.steamshipmutual.com/sites/default/files/medialibrary/files/2025%20-%20Jan%20-%20Dec%20IMB%20Piracy%20and%20Armed%20Robbery%20Report%20-%20MEDIA%20RELEASE.pdf]
- **IMB January–September 2026.** 51 incidents worldwide, the lowest since 1992. All 8 hijackings were by Somali pirates, who also held 94% of crew taken hostage. Puntland reported five *Honour 25* crew killed. [IMB Jan–Sep 2026, ICC, 15 Oct 2026, https://iccwbo.org/news-publications/news/maritime-piracy-incidents-remain-at-historic-lows-despite-somali-piracy-concerns/]
- **April 2026 hijackings.** 21 Apr off Hafun (32.5 nm), 26 Apr off Eyl (49 nm). On 23 Apr the armed guards of a ship off Garacad drove off a skiff. All the attacks were within 42 nm of the coast. [Threat Circular, Ambrey, 29 Apr 2026, https://ambrey.com/ara290426tc/]
- **Vessels held.** *Eureka* (since 2 May) and *Asana* (since 17 Jul) were still held on 29 Sep. [Maritime Executive, 29 Sep 2026, https://maritime-executive.com/article/somali-forces-free-second-tanker-held-by-pirates-for-five-months]
- **November (secondary).** JMIC rates the Somali coast/basin MODERATE, and the end of the monsoon makes offshore skiff operations more feasible. On 16 Nov an armed skiff approached a tanker about 430 nm east of Socotra (UKMTO 206-26). [gCaptain, 16 Nov 2026, https://gcaptain.com/armed-skiff-approaches-tanker-far-out-in-arabian-sea/]

## 3. Gulf of Oman / Strait of Hormuz

- **Boundary.** The same JWC area includes the Gulf and the Gulf of Oman. Bahrain, Iran, Iraq, Kuwait, Oman, Qatar and the UAE are named. [JWLA-035, as above]
- **31 May 2026.** Hormuz CRITICAL, Gulf of Oman SEVERE. Hormuz carried 3–4 transits a day, against about 138 historically. A suspected mine was reported at 26°24.3'N 056°20.6'E. [JMIC Update 052, as above]
- **From 1 Oct 2026.** More than 20 vessels were attacked in Hormuz, the Gulf and the Gulf of Oman. [Insurance Business, 14 Oct 2026, https://www.insurancebusinessmag.com/ca/news/marine/hormuz-sinking-hands-marine-war-underwriters-another-total-loss-as-attacks-surge-593542.aspx]
- **8 Nov 2026 (secondary, citing JMIC Update 112).**
  - Hormuz SEVERE, Gulf of Oman MODERATE.
  - About 162 ships were anchored off Fujairah and Khor Fakkan.
  - On 7 Nov US aircraft disabled a bulker about 18 nm east of Khor Fakkan while enforcing the blockade.

  [Palaemon, as above]

## 4. Gulf of Guinea

- **JWC.** Benin, Nigeria and Togo are named countries. The listed waters run from Togo (6°06'45"N 1°12'E) south to 0°40'S 3°00'E, then east to Cape Lopez, Gabon (0°40'S 8°42'E). [JWLA-035, as above]
- **MDAT-GoG VRA.** Shown on Admiralty Chart Q6114. Attacks have occurred from Angola to Sierra Leone. [Gulf of Guinea guidelines v2, industry associations via West P&I, Jun 2016, https://www.westpandi.com/globalassets/news/guidelines-for-owners-operators-and-masters-for-protection-against-piracy-in-the-gulf-of-guinea-region-version-2---june-2016.pdf]
- **Incidents.** 21 in 2025 (18 in 2024), with 23 crew kidnapped in four incidents. [IMB 2025, as above] 2 in January–September 2026. [IMB Jan–Sep 2026, as above]
- **Armed guards.** Private armed guards "are prevented by law from operating inside territorial waters" of coastal states. [GoG guidelines, as above]
- **Nigeria.**
  - Armed guards have been prohibited since 6 Jun 2016. Only the Navy and NIMASA may provide security.
  - Escort vessels operate under a Navy MoU.
  - The Lagos Secure Anchorage Area has been disbanded. Escorts must disengage at the anchorage or give 5 h notice, and any extended stay is capped at 24 h.

  [Nigeria guidance, Skuld, 9 Mar 2021, https://www.skuld.com/topics/port/piracy/nigeria-guidance-on-contracting-government-security-forces/]

## Armed security in practice

- **Floating armouries.** Around 2014 there were about 30, "mostly deployed in the Red Sea and the Gulf of Oman", plus one off Sri Lanka, moored at Galle naval base. [Research Note 52, Small Arms Survey, May 2015, https://smallarmssurvey.org/sites/default/files/resources/SAS-Research-Note-52.pdf]
- **Guard teams.** Guards join and leave ships at floating armouries. High-risk ships carry 6–8 guards, and guards "cannot stop missile attacks". [Lloyd's List, 12 Jan 2024, https://www.lloydslist.com/LL1147920/Red-Sea-crisis-renews-interest-in-armed-guards]
- **Suez.** Guards may transit if a flag-state-endorsed letter is sent to the SCA first. [West P&I, 8 Nov 2011, https://www.westpandi.com/news-and-resources/news/egypt-carriage-of-armed-guards-weapons-and-ammunit/] Agents hold the weapons during transit, for a USD 500 storage fee. [Skuld, 9 Apr 2015, https://www.skuld.com/topics/port/port-news/africa/egypt-rules-on-the-storage-of-weapons/]

## Costs

- **Guards.** A three-person team cost about $100–120k a month in 2011–13 and about $30k now. [Lloyd's List, 12 Jan 2024, as above] Floating armouries charged USD 500 per guard to embark or disembark (2014). [Small Arms Survey, as above]
- **War risk, Red Sea.** About 1% of ship value, up from 0.7%. [Reuters via Business Insurance, 7 Feb 2024, https://www.businessinsurance.com/us-uk-ship-investors-hit-by-soaring-red-sea-insurance-sources/] 0.3% rising to 0.7% after the Jul 2025 attacks. [Reuters via Baird Maritime, 10 Jul 2025, https://www.bairdmaritime.com/amp/story/shipping/insurance-costs-surge-in-red-sea-after-deadly-houthi-ship-attacks]
- **War risk, July 2026.** Bab el-Mandeb 0.5%, Saudi west coast without transiting the strait 0.1%, Hormuz 7.5–10%. [Al Jazeera citing S&P/Marsh, as above] Gulf tankers 6–10% in October. [Insurance Business, as above]
- **Suez dues.** LR1 tankers (60–80k dwt) measure 32,000–44,000 SCNT; no dollar toll is given. [Leth Agencies, https://lethagencies.com/1078] Laden crude tankers pay a 37% surcharge from 15 Jul 2026. [SCA Periodical 16/2026 via Sealagom, 7 Jun 2026, https://www.sealagom.com/coastal/186/message/41444/sca-periodical-16-2026-suez-canal-authority-periodical-no/] Tanker rebates were extended to 30 Jun 2026. [Vesseltracker, 4 Dec 2025, https://www.vesseltracker.com/en/news/67.html]
- **Convoys.** Northbound 05:30–11:00, southbound 23:30–05:00 (arrive at Port Said before 18:00), transit 12–16 h. [Kuehne+Nagel, Sep 2024, https://mykn.kuehne-nagel.com/news/article/passage-through-the-suez-canal-13-Sep-2024]

## Distances

- Going round the Cape "adds some 4,900 nautical miles and between 14 and 16 days" from the Middle East Gulf to Rotterdam. [Lloyd's List, 25 Jan 2024, https://www.lloydslist.com/LL1148053/More-suezmax-tankers-take-the-long-way-to-Europe-and-the-Med]
- Platts puts the Cape route 10–14 days longer from the Gulf to UK/Continent. [Platts via Hellenic Shipping News, 3 Dec 2025, https://www.hellenicshippingnews.com/?p=1115010]
- UNCTAD gives Ras Tanura → Rotterdam as 10,358 km via Suez and 17,975 km via the Cape, about 5,593 nm and 9,706 nm (my conversion). [UNCTAD via Hellenic Shipping News, 26 Feb 2024, https://www.hellenicshippingnews.com/?p=1029524]
